# Why this case

A functional analyst must turn an ambiguous request into behaviour that developers can build, testers can validate and support can explain.

This case links [discovery](../docs/01-discovery/interview-notes.md) to [business rules](../docs/03-analysis/business-rules.md), [stories](../docs/04-backlog/user-stories.md), [models](../docs/05-models/state-machine.md), [interfaces](../docs/06-interfaces/edi-amendment.md) and [handover](../docs/08-handover/kb-article.md). [Review decisions](../docs/03-analysis/review-decisions.md) show corrections across those artefacts.

It demonstrates analysis structure, consistency and design reasoning. It does not demonstrate real stakeholder facilitation, production logistics experience or achieved business results.
