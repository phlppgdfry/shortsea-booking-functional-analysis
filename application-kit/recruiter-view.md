# Recruiter view

> Independent portfolio case study based on a fictional organisation. It contains no confidential information from any real employer or the hiring company. All figures are illustrative assumptions, not real company data.

**Problem:** a fictional RoRo operator applies inconsistent sailing-change rules across portal, EDI and agents.

**Evidence:** simulated discovery, workshop plan, 18 rules, 10 stories, 27 executable decision examples, process/state/data models, interfaces, UAT plan and support handover.

**Review:** the case resolves a deadline contradiction, separates receipt eligibility from execution safety and separates booking status from notification delivery.

**Limits:** no real interviews, production deployment or measured benefits are claimed.
