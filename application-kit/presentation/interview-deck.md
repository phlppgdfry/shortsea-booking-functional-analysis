## 2. The booking problem

The portal stops changes 24 hours early.
EDI overwrites bookings without shared checks.
Agents use remembered rules and spreadsheets.
The terminal may still show the old sailing.

## 3. Discovery and decisions

Simulated interviews expose contradictory cut-offs.
An event timeline makes disagreement visible.
The analyst prepares options and consequences.
Decision owners validate the business policy.

## 4. Deadline and execution

At exactly T-30, changes and approvals close.
Receipt time determines cut-off eligibility.
Current status, capacity and time control execution.
A timely request can arrive too late to execute.

## 5. Booking and delivery status

Applied means the booking commit succeeded.
The commit durably queues notifications.
Delivery can remain queued, retrying or failed.
Support replays and reconciles the notification.
