Independent fictional portfolio case

Late booking amendments

One policy across portal, EDI and agents, with testable rules and support recovery.

Challenge

Inconsistent cut-offs, overwrites and delayed terminal updates make booking changes difficult to explain.

Analysis

Simulated discovery and event storming, 18 rules, 10 stories, models and interfaces. 27 executable decision examples and 43 automated checks.

Review decisions

At T-30 changes and approvals close. Receipt determines eligibility; current time/status/capacity determine execution. Applied and notification delivery have separate states.

Delivery

Proposed pilot, UAT-01–16, support training, feature flag and reconciliation. Real interviews, UAT and production rollout are not claimed.

Illustrative targets

Amendment incidents 6/month to 0; phone share 35% to below 10%; fee evidence 100%. These are hypotheses, not achieved results.
