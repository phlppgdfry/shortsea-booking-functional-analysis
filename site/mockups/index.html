What to notice

  • Full sailings are shown but cannot be selected; the next sailing with space is highlighted, so the customer does not need to call.
  • Each sailing shows until when it is "on time" — the cut-off is visible before the customer asks.
  • The customs warning appears before confirming, and again in the e-mail.
  • A rejection is shown as information with a next step, never as "an error occurred".
